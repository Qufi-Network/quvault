    Post quantum

    Security for the world after classical cryptography.

    The security landscape is changing. Veyns is designed with post quantum cryptographic protection in mind, helping institutions prepare for a future where today's cryptographic assumptions may no longer be sufficient.

    1. ClassicalRSA and elliptic curve
    2. HybridClassical and lattice together
    3. Post QuantumLattice based signatures and encapsulation

    Veyns Quantum Vault uses ML-DSA-65 for authorisation signatures and ML-KEM-768 alongside X25519 for key encapsulation.
